## Per-target reframe summary

### NEE (primary)
- Lead thesis: **functional recovery ≠ greenness recovery; ecosystem resilience must be measured in trait space**
- Connect explicitly to trait ecology lineage: Wright 2004 (LES) → Díaz 2016 (global spectrum) → Violle 2012 / Siefert 2015 (ITV) → resilience theory (Pimm/Holling/Hodgson)
- Sell ITV as a landscape-scale state variable observable for the first time
- Demote climate-attribution from organizing argument to one of several use cases
- Compress sensor/CHIME-SBG content; frame as "ecological opportunity," not "mission window"

### Nature Plants (fallback 1)
- Strengthen plant-physiology mechanism for each anchored trait (LMA→LES, N→Rubisco, lignin→defense/turnover, chlorophyll→light harvesting)
- Use Jetz et al. 2016 Nat Plants as template
- Keep some EO content; Nature Plants accepts satellite-scale work when plant-level mechanism is well-articulated

### GCB (fallback 2)
- Minimal change from NEE version (or even keep current 2026-05-22 NCC-framed version with light reframe)
- Emphasize climate stress + ecosystem response + trait fingerprints (GCB's bread-and-butter)
- Fastest landing; protect 2027 Q1 publication window

### One Earth (fallback 3)
- Strengthen operational + policy + Earth-system framing
- Keep current European forest illustration as full demonstrator
- See `target/OneEarth_pitch.md`
