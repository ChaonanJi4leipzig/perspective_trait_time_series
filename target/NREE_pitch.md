# Presubmission pitch: Nature Reviews Earth & Environment

## Proposed title

From greenness to function in ecosystem monitoring

## Article type

Perspective proposal, approximately 4,000--4,500 words with five display items.

## Core argument

Ecosystem monitoring has gained long temporal memory from spectral-index and multispectral archives, while climate extremes require functional observables that can distinguish stress pathways, recovery and legacy effects. Imaging spectroscopy can retrieve and anchor biochemical and optical plant traits, but current hyperspectral observations are sparse in time and should not be framed as a direct source of detailed three-dimensional canopy structure. We propose a framework for moving from trait snapshots to functional time series by combining imaging-spectroscopy trait anchors, dense Sentinel-2 and Landsat-era archives, and complementary structural observations from LiDAR, SAR and InSAR.

## Why now

Spaceborne imaging spectroscopy is entering an operational phase through PRISMA, EnMAP, EMIT and upcoming CHIME/SBG-class missions. At the same time, ecological monitoring increasingly needs functional indicators for drought response, resilience and Earth-system model evaluation. Future CHIME/SBG observations will make trait snapshots more systematic, but long-term monitoring still needs to connect them to existing Sentinel-2 and Landsat-era archives. The field needs a Perspective that defines how sparse trait retrievals can become continuous monitoring products without overstating what spectroscopy directly observes.

## What is new

The manuscript does not review all hyperspectral methods. It treats trait retrieval as essential and active, then asks how retrieved trait snapshots can be organized into functional time series with uncertainty, provenance, domain-of-applicability masks and ecological validation. A key boundary is the distinction between spectrally anchored traits and traits inferred through trait covariation, temporal context, canopy state and ecological correlation.

## Proof-of-concept role

A European forest testbed is included as a compact proof-of-concept showing early reconstruction of selected hyperspectral-derived traits from Sentinel-2 temporal context. It is not presented as a final benchmark, and the internal project name should not be foregrounded in the manuscript.
