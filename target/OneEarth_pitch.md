# Presubmission pitch: One Earth

## Proposed title

Turning spectral snapshots into ecosystem trait cubes

## Article type

Perspective.

## Core argument

Operational ecosystem monitoring needs functional variables that can inform climate adaptation, conservation and Earth system modelling. Imaging spectroscopy provides trait-rich snapshots, while multispectral archives provide temporal continuity. Trait data cubes offer a route from remote-sensing data streams to actionable ecosystem intelligence, provided that uncertainty and domain-of-applicability are reported as core product layers.

## One Earth fit

This version should emphasize monitoring infrastructure, climate resilience, conservation planning and policy-facing products. It can give more space to the EcoSpectra proof-of-concept and roadmap than the NREE/TREE versions.

## EcoSpectra role

EcoSpectra is framed as a European forest demonstrator for reconstructing selected hyperspectral-derived traits from Sentinel-2 temporal context.

## Roadmap emphasis

1. Open spectra-trait-time benchmarks.
2. Cross-mission harmonization.
3. Uncertainty and domain masks.
4. Connection to climate drivers, flux towers and ecological networks.
5. Co-designed products for scientists and decision-makers.
