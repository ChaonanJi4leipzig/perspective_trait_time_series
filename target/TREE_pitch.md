# Presubmission pitch: Trends in Ecology & Evolution

## Proposed title

From greenness to function in ecosystem monitoring

## Article type

Opinion / Review-style Perspective proposal.

## Core argument

Trait ecology has transformed how ecologists interpret ecosystem function, but most large-scale monitoring still observes greenness rather than traits. Imaging spectroscopy can retrieve plant traits, and dense multispectral archives can support temporal reconstruction. We argue that functional time series can move ecosystem monitoring from greenness anomalies to functional anomaly analysis.

## TREE fit

The manuscript is written for ecologists rather than remote-sensing specialists. It focuses on stress syndromes, resistance, recovery, legacy effects, dynamic functional diversity and model benchmarking. Remote sensing is treated as the observing infrastructure that makes these ecological variables measurable across space and time.

## Proof-of-concept role

A European forest testbed appears as a short proof-of-concept demonstrating feasibility. The manuscript does not claim that this testbed has already solved operational trait monitoring.
