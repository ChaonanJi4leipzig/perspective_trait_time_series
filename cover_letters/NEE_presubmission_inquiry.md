The working title is:

**"From greenness recovery to trait-space recovery in a changing climate."**

The Perspective addresses a conceptual gap left by recent NEE Perspectives on remote sensing for biodiversity (Cavender-Bares et al. 2022; Skidmore et al. 2021): while those works argued for the integration of remote sensing with field ecology and prioritized which biodiversity variables to observe, neither addressed *what ecological state variable spaceborne trait observation should produce* as climate change reshapes ecosystem function. We argue that this variable is the dynamic **trait-state trajectory** of communities through multidimensional functional trait space, and propose **continuous functional observables** as the measurement framework that makes such trajectories—and intraspecific trait variability (ITV) at landscape scale—tractable as ecosystem-scale state variables.

The Perspective is anchored to a concrete ecological puzzle posed by the 2022 European drought-and-heat event: by mid-2023, satellite greenness anomalies over most of drought-affected Europe had relaxed to near-baseline, classifying the forests as "recovered," yet ICP Forests crown-condition assessments and FLUXNET physiological measurements from the same stands continued to record altered leaf economics, depressed nitrogen and lingering hydraulic damage. We argue that this is a conceptual problem of state-space choice, not a sensor problem—and that the EnMAP-era (April 2022 onward) hyperspectral archive over Europe now makes the alternative state space, trait space, empirically tractable for the first time at a continental scale.

The manuscript makes three connected theoretical claims:

1. Trait-state trajectories extend the leaf economics spectrum and global plant form–function spectrum from static species-mean axes to dynamic, intraspecific descriptors at landscape scale (Wright 2004, Díaz 2016, Reich 2014, Joswig 2022, Bruelheide 2018).

2. Intraspecific trait variability becomes a landscape-scale, time-resolved ecological state variable (Violle 2012, Siefert 2015, Westerband 2021)—a step that previous remote-sensing perspectives have not developed.

3. Resilience theory (Pimm 1984, Holling 1973, Hodgson 2015, Donohue 2016) is operationalized in trait space: functional recovery is return of trait distributions to the pre-disturbance state within uncertainty; functional legacy is the persistent offset after greenness has returned to baseline. Box 1 of the manuscript provides falsifiable predictions for the trait fingerprints of five common climate stressors.

The manuscript is approximately 4,800 words, with four main-text display items (a conceptual trait-space figure, a three-pillar measurement-framework figure, a trait readiness table, and a trait-space trajectory figure including a 2022 drought test case) and a single box of falsifiable stress-fingerprint predictions. Supplementary materials include extended principles for credible functional observables, mission-specific calibration considerations, and underlying trait retrieval performance metrics from the European forest illustration.

## Strategic notes for Chaonan

- This letter is the **single most important** document in the submission pipeline. Cavender-Bares is the most natural reviewer for this paper; keeping her absent from the review table is the highest-leverage editorial intervention available.
- You are the corresponding author. The three senior cc's give the inquiry seniority signal, but it is **your** Perspective and the letter should sound like that.
- The "non-preferred reviewers" request is unusual but not improper; editors honor it most of the time when the rationale is intellectual proximity (rather than personal conflict).
- If the editor pushes back on the non-preferred request, fall back to a softer "We would appreciate if the manuscript could be reviewed by colleagues whose own work does not directly precede ours in this journal, to enable independent assessment."
- If the editor declines presubmission entirely with a "submit it and we'll see," the next step is **full submission only after the parallel Cavender-Bares outreach in `outreach/cavenderbares_invitation.md` has been attempted**.
- If you do not receive a reply within 14 calendar days, send a brief polite follow-up — do not assume silence is decline.

## Why this configuration (Chaonan-direct with three senior cc) over Mahecha-direct

- **Authenticity**: You own this Perspective intellectually; the editor sees that directly.
- **Modern NEE practice**: Corresponding-author-led inquiries are standard; senior cc's provide the seniority signal without obscuring leadership.
- **Faster downstream**: All review-related correspondence reaches you directly, no Mahecha-relay delay.
- **No relationship cost**: Mahecha does not have to spend his editor-time-capital on a paper where his contribution is co-authorship rather than lead; that capital is preserved for future joint work.
- **Trade-off accepted**: Mahecha's possible direct personal connection to the editor is partially substituted by his explicit cc presence and named recipient of any reply.
