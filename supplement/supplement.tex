\section*{Densification and retrospective reconstruction}

The proof-of-concept is designed to test how sparse hyperspectral-derived trait labels can be linked to dense Sentinel-2 temporal context. We use \textit{densification} to refer to filling temporal gaps between trait anchors within the contemporary satellite record. We use \textit{retrospective reconstruction} to refer to cautious inference of trait-relevant functional states through existing multispectral archives, including Sentinel-2 since 2015 and, trait by trait where defensible, Landsat-era observations.

The final supplement should report which traits are suitable for each task, the input sensors used, validation splits, uncertainty estimates and known failure modes. It should also distinguish observed hyperspectral trait anchors, densified estimates within the contemporary record and retrospective predictions outside the direct hyperspectral archive.
