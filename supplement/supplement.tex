\section*{Supplementary Note 1. Reporting practices for trait-state observables}

The main text lists six reporting practices. Here they are expanded into candidate requirements for product papers and data releases.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Define each trait with physical units, biological support and ecological interpretation before fitting the model.
\item Distinguish the hyperspectral label step from the Sentinel-2 or Landsat reconstruction step. Direct/indirect status applies to the label step; every dense multispectral time-series value remains a learned reconstruction.
\item Report retrieval uncertainty, reconstruction uncertainty and transfer uncertainty separately where possible.
\item Attach domain-of-applicability masks and provenance classes to every ecological claim.
\item Stratify validation by trait, site, year, season, vegetation type and disturbance context; pooled random-split metrics are not sufficient for recovery claims.
\item Test trait covariance and trajectory direction against ecological expectations, not only against held-out labels.
\end{enumerate}
